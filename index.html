What this is (and is not)

A film-style look for photos that are already processed (JPEG/PNG). The colour comes from a datasheet-based simulation of a film negative printed on print stock (Spectral Film LUT, MIT licence), plus halation and grain made here.

It is not Chromagraph and not Steve Yedlin's transform. Chromagraph develops RAW sensor data with its own proprietary pipeline; this app does not read RAW at all.

Photos are processed on this device. Nothing is uploaded and there is no analytics. Saved JPEGs carry no EXIF or location data. Originals are never changed.

HEIC: works only if this browser can decode it. RAW (DNG etc.): not supported.
