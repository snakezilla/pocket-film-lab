About Film Lab

Film Lab gives your photos the colour and texture of motion-picture and still film. The colour comes from a datasheet-based simulation of film negatives printed on print stock (Spectral Film LUT, MIT licence); halation and grain are made here.

In the iOS app, RAW and ProRAW photos are developed natively from the sensor data. JPEG, PNG and HEIC photos get the same looks on already-processed pixels. It is not Chromagraph and not Steve Yedlin’s transform.

Photos are processed on this device. Nothing is uploaded and there is no analytics. Saved photos carry no location or camera data. Originals are never changed.
