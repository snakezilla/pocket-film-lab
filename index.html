About Silver Hour

Silver Hour gives your photos the colours and grain of old-school film.

Everything happens right here on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, there are no accounts, and nothing is tracked. Your originals stay just as they are, and saved photos don’t carry your location.

Film colours made with the open-source Spectral Film LUT project (MIT licence).
