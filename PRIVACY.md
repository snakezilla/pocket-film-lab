# Silver Hour privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Silver Hour gives your photos a film look. It is built so your photos stay yours.

- **No data is collected.** Silver Hour has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
- **No network calls for your photos.** The app does not upload, send or sync your photos anywhere. All processing happens on your device.
- **Photos are read only when you pick them.** Silver Hour sees only the photos you choose in the photo picker or in Files. It cannot browse your library.
- **Photos are written only when you tap Save.** Silver Hour asks for "Add Photos Only" access. It adds your finished photos to your library and cannot read or change your other photos. Your originals are never changed.
- **Location and camera details are removed.** Saved and shared photos do not carry GPS location or camera information (EXIF metadata) from the original.
- **Sharing is up to you.** If you tap Share, iOS shows its share sheet, and the photo goes only where you send it.

Note: if a photo you pick is stored in iCloud, iOS may download it to your device so the app can open it. Apple's photo picker does this, not Silver Hour.
